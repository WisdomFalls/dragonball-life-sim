# External component concepts → Mortal Coil 04A morphology

XV2-style names such as FaceBase, FaceForehead, FaceEye, FaceNose and FaceEar are useful implementation boundaries when present, but they are not a one-to-one replacement for Mortal Coil physiology. FaceBase can inform HeadMorphology and broad jaw/chin planes; FaceForehead informs forehead/brow projection; FaceEye informs EyeMorphology, spacing and angle; FaceNose informs NoseMorphology; FaceEar informs EarMorphology. Mouth geometry may be embedded in FaceBase or another facial mesh and therefore maps only by landmarks, not by assumed object names.

Mortal Coil keeps continuous inherited phenotype separate from curated renderer buckets and authored asset IDs. External components are scaffolds/specimens. They do not become “the Saiyan face,” and their material, contour, palette, and pixel language remain outside the donor contract. Expression is current state; scars and missing anatomy are acquired BODY history.

The preferred eventual seam is a measured CanonicalHeadProxy with stable head/face/hair anchors. Luceus registration requires crown, hairline, temples, rear skull, ear occlusion and neck attachment, but no donor is declared canonical before measurement.
