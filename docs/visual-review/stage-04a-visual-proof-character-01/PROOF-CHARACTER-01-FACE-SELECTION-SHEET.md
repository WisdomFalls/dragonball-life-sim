# Proof Character 01 — Face Identity Selection Sheet (04B input)

Choose one descriptive identity before face authoring. These are compact morphology decisions, not personality traits.

- **HeadMorphology:** balanced adult cranial envelope; choose width/depth bucket and forehead height.
- **JawMorphology:** choose taper: broad, balanced, or narrow; record mandibular width.
- **ChinMorphology:** choose projection and vertical height; avoid psychological labels.
- **BrowMorphology:** choose supraorbital projection and arch; expression remains current state.
- **EyeMorphology:** choose shape (`sharp`, `round`, `narrow`, `wide`), spacing, and neutral angle.
- **NoseMorphology:** choose bridge width, projection, and tip plane.
- **MouthMorphology:** choose width and lip-plane thickness; neutral resting state.
- **EarMorphology:** choose size, angle, and attachment; retain bilateral anatomical sides.
- **FacialProportionProfile:** adult U7 Saiyan, Balanced/#5A head-to-body relationship.
- **PhysicalSkinPhenotype:** inherited skin tone and shading profile; scars/marks remain acquired history.

04B should author one male adult face from these choices, then test portrait, full-body, and gameplay simplifications against the same identity.
