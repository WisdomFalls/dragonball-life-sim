# Visual Redesign Roadmap

## Stage 1 — Appearance data stabilisation — Complete

A presentation resolver now combines saved appearance, worn equipment, injuries, marks, afterlife state and transformations without altering simulation data.

## Stage 2 — Art direction — Complete

`CHARACTER_ART_DIRECTION.md` locks the approved Dragon Ball-inspired polished pixel-art language, original-character rule, portrait framing and asset requirements.

## Stage 3 — Renderer contract — Complete

`src/ui/portrait-assets.js` produces one deterministic layer plan for compact sprites and full character sheets. The existing SVG remains the fallback until a complete approved asset plan is available.

## Stage 4 — First production character family — In progress

Build and validate clean transparent assets for the original horned humanoid-alien family, then connect its compact sprite and sheet portrait to the asset renderer.

## Stage 5 — Playable and generated body families — Planned

Add humanoid, Namekian, Frost Demon, Majin, Bio-Android, Yardratian, Kryllian and Great Ape rigs. A body family is rendering anatomy, not a gameplay race.

## Stage 6 — Modular visual content — Planned

Add approved face, hair/crest, clothing, armour, item, injury, aura and pose layers.

## Stage 7 — Transformation rigs — Planned

Add authored large, giant and beast rigs for forms whose silhouette changes materially.

## Stage 8 — Screen rollout — Planned

Use compact sprites in the life feed and combat; use larger near-full-body portraits on player and NPC sheets.

## Stage 9 — Tactical combat presentation — Planned

Build animated turn-based encounters using the visual foundation: movement, flight, clashes, terrain damage, injuries and equipment damage.
